Binance · Security Incident

$6.34B in digital assets exposed — a hack that started with an AI coding tool

On 29 October 2026 Binance disclosed a sophisticated, multi-stage security incident. An AI-assisted developer environment became the entry point; internal wallet signing systems were later reached; roughly $2.85B was frozen mid-flight.

  1. June 2026

    Supply chain compromise

    An engineer installed a malicious Homebrew package while setting up a popular AI coding tool. Binance detected the anomaly, replaced the device, and rotated credentials — but the wallet team had already been exposed.

  2. Sept – Oct 2026

    Social engineering & AI tool springboard

    A fake recruitment pipeline led to a malicious repo and malware; the attacker later used an internal AI tool to pivot toward wallet infrastructure.

  3. Oct 29, 2026 · 17:15 UTC

    Signing service reached

    Stolen credentials let the attacker deploy a container and trigger a malicious script against Binance's wallet signing operations.

  4. Oct 29, 2026 · 20:05–20:09 UTC

    Funds move

    233.46M XRP, ~970,500 SOL, 44.2M OP and later 18,270 BTC were sent toward attacker addresses; the final unauthorized transfer was 2,961.9 ETH.

  5. Containment

    Freeze & revoke

    Binance suspended withdrawals and froze roughly $2.85B in assets; the attacker used EIP-7702 delegation manipulation via 50 signing transactions to keep moving funds.

  6. Oct 30, 2026 · 00:10 UTC

    Containment complete

    The final ETH transfer settled at 23:56 UTC; Binance cleared the delegation via a zero-address authorization. ~98% of impacted assets were replenished as of Oct 31.
