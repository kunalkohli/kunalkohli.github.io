The attack chain

Six stages, five months, one trusted tool.

01 / 06
Stage 01 · Recon

Engineer A installs a tampered AI coding tool. Binance cleans the device, but the attacker now knows the wallet team.

Stage 02 · Lure

A fake recruiter runs two interview rounds and sends a take-home assignment. Its setup code runs on Engineer B’s work laptop.

Stage 03 · Foothold

The malware replaces pyenv, a trusted developer tool, with a same-name copy that opens a remote shell.

Stage 04 · Pivot

An internal AI tool takes the attacker to two more engineers. Engineer A has a container deployment token on his laptop.

Stage 05 · Breach

A malicious container reaches the internal signing service and triggers transfers outside the normal withdrawal path.

Stage 06 · Drain

Ten chains are emptied in about 20 minutes. Ethereum’s EIP-7702 delegation keeps funds moving after withdrawals are paused.

Five months · six steps · no private key stolen

Source: Binance initial findings, 3 Nov 2026.
